The Evolving Landscape of Business Technology

A durable IT Infrastructure forms the backbone of modern enterprise operations. However, the landscape is rapidly evolving, driven by powerful Emerging Technologies. Our research, drawing significantly from studies like the AI Adoption Reports and Technological analysis, explores this dynamic interplay.

Technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), advanced data analytics, and cloud platforms are no longer niche concepts but are becoming integral to business strategy, efficiency, and competitiveness. This presentation examines key trends, adoption patterns, and the necessary infrastructural considerations for utilizing these transformative technologies.

Key AI Adoption Insights (PwC 2023 Study):

  • Globally, around 35% of companies reported actively using AI, with another 42% exploring its potential.
  • Key drivers for AI adoption include increasing accessibility, potential for cost reduction, and rising competitive pressure.
  • Significant barriers remain, including limited AI skills/expertise (cited by 34%), high implementation costs (29%), and the complexity of data and integration (24-25%).
  • The Generative AI market showed rapid growth potential, estimated at $8B in 2022.
  • Future adoption growth areas include Synthetic Data generation, Data-centric AI approaches, and Edge AI deployments.

Note: Data sourced from PwC "AI Adoption in the Business World" report, April 2023.
